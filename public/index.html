Turn your phone into a wireless webcam.

Use your phone camera on your PC or laptop without a cable.

Use Phone as Webcam I'm on the PC

Stream your phone camera to your PC over WebRTC, then use OBS Virtual Camera to make it available to Zoom, Discord, Google Meet, Teams, and other apps.

How it works

  1. Open Phebcam on your phone and start the camera
  2. Open the viewer on your PC and enter the session code
  3. Add the viewer to OBS and enable Virtual Camera

Camera access requires browser permission. Video is sent through a WebRTC peer connection directly between your phone and PC. Phebcam doesn't guarantee zero latency, connectivity across every network, or production-grade reliability — see the project README for current limitations.
